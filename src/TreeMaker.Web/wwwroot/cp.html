Fold a crease pattern

Open a crease pattern, check its lines, and see it folded flat. Vector files (.svg, .fold) are read exactly. Images (.png, .jpg) are traced: red lines are mountains, blue lines valleys, dark lines creases or the border. Check the traced lines before folding. This page is separate from the session and keeps nothing.

1. Crease pattern

How the lines are read

SVG: every line, polyline, polygon, rect and straight path. Red strokes are mountains and blue strokes valleys. Grey or black strokes on the outline are the border, and the others are creases with no mountain or valley given. A title containing "mountain" or "valley" also sets it. FOLD: edges_assignment M, V, B, F and U.

Images: the pixels are sorted by colour, straight lines are found in each colour (a Hough transform), and their ends are joined and snapped. Click a line in the drawing to change it: mountain → valley → unassigned → flat → removed.

3. Folded flat

    The folded faces are drawn see-through, the paper's front in one colour and its back in another. Layer order is not worked out. Red rings on the lines mark vertices that fail Kawasaki's or Maekawa's theorem.
